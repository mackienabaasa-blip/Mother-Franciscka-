Our Services

We offer a wide range of medical services to meet the healthcare needs of our community.

Medical Services We Offer

Outpatient Services

General consultations, diagnosis and treatment for patients who do not need to be admitted. Our outpatient department handles common illnesses, injuries and follow-up visits.

Maternity Services

Comprehensive maternity care including antenatal check-ups, safe delivery by trained midwives, and postnatal care for both mother and baby.

Laboratory Services

Modern laboratory testing for accurate diagnosis including blood tests, malaria testing, HIV testing, urinalysis and other essential diagnostic services.

Immunization

Routine immunization and vaccination programmes for children and adults following the national immunization schedule to prevent common diseases.

Ambulance Services

24/7 emergency ambulance services for patient transfers and emergency response. Our ambulance is equipped to handle critical situations.

Pharmacy Services

A fully stocked pharmacy providing prescribed medicines and health supplies to our patients at affordable prices.

Eye Clinic

Eye examination, diagnosis and treatment of common eye conditions. We help patients maintain good vision and eye health.

Ultrasound Scan

Ultrasound scanning services for pregnancy monitoring and diagnostic imaging to help doctors identify and treat medical conditions.

Counselling

Professional counselling services for patients dealing with health-related concerns, including HIV/AIDS counselling, mental health support and guidance.

Inpatient Care

Patients who need to be admitted for close monitoring and treatment are cared for in our wards by our experienced nursing team.

Maternity Ward

Our maternity ward provides a safe and comfortable environment for mothers and newborns.

Emergency & Ambulance

Our ambulance service is available around the clock for emergencies. If you need emergency medical assistance, call us immediately.
